# PRODUCT.md

## Product

**Financial Tracker** — personal receipt expense tracker for item-level spending visibility.

## Who

A single primary user (owner) today; multi-user ready for a future public launch.

## Problem

Bank statement totals hide what was actually bought. After a layoff, the user needs line-item truth (store, date, qty, price, category, tax) to find where money goes.

## Mode

Application surfaces are **Operate** (task completion): upload, review flagged receipts, browse category totals.

## Jobs to be done

1. Upload a receipt photo/PDF image and get parsed line items automatically.
2. See Ontario-aware tax per line (zero-rated groceries vs taxable).
3. Fix flagged / wrong extractions lines after save.
4. Browse spend by category over a date range.

## Non-goals (v1)

Budgets, Google Drive watch, vision LLMs, OAuth, AI spend advice, phone-native capture.

## Success

Upload a grocery receipt → mostly correct lines with categories and Ontario tax → category totals visible → flagged misses editable.
